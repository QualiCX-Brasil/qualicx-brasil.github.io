Contact Center Quality Calculators

Five calculations every quality team should run before making decisions about people and processes. Everything runs in your browser: no data is sent anywhere.

André Luiz Maciel · About the author · ORCID 0009-0000-1747-1392

1. How much does your monitoring really tell you?

Enter how many calls were evaluated and how many failed. The Wilson interval shows the likely range of the true failure rate.

95% interval (Wilson)

2. How many evaluations do you need?

To estimate a rate within the margin of error you want (95% confidence).

3. What is the chance of catching a behaviour?

If a behaviour happens in a share of calls, what is the chance it shows up at least once in your sample?

Chance it appears at least once

4. Do your analysts score alike? (calibration)

Two analysts score the same calls independently. Cohen's kappa measures agreement beyond chance.

Method and references

Wilson score interval (Wilson, 1927); sample size by normal approximation n = z²·p(1−p)/e²; detection probability 1 − (1 − p)ⁿ; Cohen's kappa (Cohen, 1960) with the Landis and Koch (1977) scale. Results are statistical estimates and do not replace managerial judgement.
